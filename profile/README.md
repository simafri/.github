# Simafri

> Lead the way. Simafri, your technical ally, walks alongside you.

Simafri is the technical ally of SMBs: since 2012, it creates, hosts and maintains their website, their professional email, their management software, their custom applications and their servers, and protects them from cyberattacks, so the technology keeps running without them having to look after it.

## Six products

- **[Serenity Website](https://www.simafri.com/en/website-creation/)**: a website created, hosted, secured and kept up to date, with a domain name registered in the customer's name and a Simafri Suite account included.
- **[Simafri Suite](https://www.simafri.com/en/suite/)**: professional email, calendar, contacts and files on the customer's own domain, sold per account with unlimited users.
- **[Simafri ERP](https://www.simafri.com/en/erp/)**: quotes, invoices, credit notes, customers, suppliers, purchases, banking and stock, sold per company with unlimited users and contacts.
- **[Pléiade](https://www.simafri.com/en/custom-software/)**: software built to a customer's own needs, then hosted, updated and supported month after month like everything else Simafri runs.
- **[Simafri VPS](https://www.simafri.com/en/vps/)**: Linux servers on Intel Xeon processors, ECC memory and mirrored NVMe SSDs, backed up every night, with Full-Stack Assistance included: diagnosis and guidance from the operating system down to the application code. Also available as [managed VPS](https://www.simafri.com/en/managed-vps/), run end to end by Simafri.
- **[Managed cybersecurity](https://www.simafri.com/en/managed-cybersecurity/)**: continuous monitoring, email authentication (SPF, DKIM, DMARC), lookalike domain watch and incident response, plus [security audits](https://www.simafri.com/en/security-audit/) and [IT fleet management](https://www.simafri.com/en/it-fleet-management/).

## How Simafri works

- Everything is managed and ongoing, with no commitment period: the website, the email, the management software and the servers are set up, kept up to date, secured and supported by Simafri technicians.
- In business since 2012 and founded by [Simon Vasconcelos Lee](https://simonvasconceloslee.com/), with customers in 42 countries and offices in the United States, the Czech Republic and Madagascar.
- What Simafri builds for a customer belongs to that customer: the website, the domain name and the data.
- Priced per account and per company rather than per seat, so adding a person to the team costs nothing.
- The [Simafri Foundation](https://www.simafri.com/en/free-website-for-nonprofits/) gives eligible non-profit associations a website at no cost, created, hosted, secured and kept up to date by Simafri, and owned by the association.
